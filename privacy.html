Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

The short version

Ys Learning Lab is a set of free worksheet generators. Every worksheet is built inside your own browser. The settings you choose, the names you type into the Title or Teacher fields, and the PDFs you generate are never sent to us or stored on any server we run. We don't have a server, accounts, or a database.

The only third party that sets cookies on this site is Google, which serves the advertisements that keep the tools free. Details are below.

Information stored in your browser

  • Worksheet settings. The Japanese Kana Worksheet Generator remembers your last settings and your five most recent generations in your browser's localStorage so you can reload them later. This data stays on your device. You can clear it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.
  • Generated PDFs. When you press Generate, the finished PDF is passed to the viewer tab through your browser's IndexedDB storage. It is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Answer-key QR codes. A printed answer-key QR code contains the answers themselves inside the link. Scanning it opens a page that decodes those answers on your phone. Nothing is looked up on a server.

Children

These worksheets are made for children to use, usually with a parent or teacher. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included, because the tools don't collect personal information at all. Names typed into the worksheet header only ever appear on the PDF you print.
