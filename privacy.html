Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Overview

Ys Learning Lab is a set of free worksheet generators. Every worksheet is built inside your own browser. The settings you choose, the names you type into the Title or Teacher fields, and the PDFs you generate are never sent to us or stored on any server we run. We don't have a server, accounts, or a database.

The only third party that sets cookies on this site is Google, which serves the advertisements that keep the tools free. Details are below.

Information stored in your browser

  • Worksheet settings. The Kana Conversion Quiz remembers your last settings and your five most recent generations in your browser's localStorage so you can reload them later. This data stays on your device. You can clear it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.
  • Tap Practice setup. Kana Tap Practice remembers the setup you last used (which kana, how many choices, and so on) in your browser's localStorage. Scores and answers are not saved. This data stays on your device and can be cleared the same way.
  • Generated PDFs. When you press Generate, the finished PDF is passed to the viewer tab through your browser's IndexedDB storage. It is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Answer-key QR codes. A printed answer-key QR code contains the answers themselves inside the link. Scanning it opens a page that decodes those answers on your phone. Nothing is looked up on a server.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect basic technical information, such as IP addresses, in its server logs for security and reliability purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Optional tips (Ko-fi)

The Support page shows a button for our page on Ko-fi, a separate service where you can leave an optional tip. To draw the button, that page loads a small script and images from Ko-fi's servers and a font from Google Fonts, so those services can receive your browser's IP address and basic request details when the page opens, even if you never click the button. The button opens Ko-fi in a new tab. This site never sees your payment details, and anything you enter on Ko-fi is handled under Ko-fi's own terms and privacy policy.

Contact form

If you choose to send a message through our contact form, which runs on Google Forms, we receive the name, email address (optional), and message you enter. We use them only to reply to you and to improve the site. We don't share them or add you to any mailing list. Google processes form submissions under the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

These worksheets are made for children to use, usually with a parent or teacher. The worksheet tools themselves don't collect personal information from anyone, children included. Names typed into the worksheet header only ever appear on the PDF you print. The contact form is intended for parents and teachers.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? See the Contact page.
