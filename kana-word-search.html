    How to play

    1. Look for words. Many words are hidden in the grid, more than you need. Every word runs in a straight line.
    2. Tap the letters one by one. Tap the first letter, then the letter beside it in the same line, and keep going. Your letters appear above. Tap a picked letter again to take it, and the letters after it, back.
    3. Press Submit. A word only counts when you press Submit, so まつり and つり are different answers even though one hides inside the other. Any word from the list counts, and a found word turns its letters green.
    4. Clear the puzzle. You clear it when you have found as many words as Words to find says. Longer words are worth more points, so a long word is the smart pick when you can see one. Letters of a found word can be used again, but a word can only score once.
    5. Stuck? Hint marks the first letter of a hidden word you have not found yet and costs 1 point.

    Options

    • Letters in the grid: from 4 × 4 to 10 × 10. A bigger grid is harder to search.
    • Hidden words: how many words are hidden on purpose, from 3 to 15 (10 to start). A small grid may have room for fewer. Other list words may turn up by chance, and they count too.
    • Directions: Across and down reads left to right and top to bottom. Plus diagonals adds diagonal lines, and Every direction also allows words that run backwards.

    Tips for finding words faster

    A grid full of kana can look like a jumble at first. These habits make the words stand out:

    • Think of a word, then look for it. Pick an everyday word such as ねこ or ほし, find its first kana in the grid, and check the letters touching it for the second one.
    • Start from one kana. Choose any kana and read outwards from it in each direction the puzzle allows. Ask yourself which kana could come next in a real word.
    • Read rows backwards too. With Every direction on, a word can run from right to left or from bottom to top, so read each row both ways.
    • Say each word aloud. Reading a word out loud when you find it ties the letters to the sound, which is the point of the practice.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where do the words come from?

    The hidden words come from the same short list of everyday hiragana words as the other kana tools. Any other everyday word you find in the grid counts too, from a longer list of common words. The filler letters are checked so that they don't spell anything that doesn't belong in a classroom.

    Why are there more hidden words than I need to find?

    So you can choose. Each word scores one point per letter, so finding the long words gets you to a high score with the same number of words. A word inside another word, such as りす inside きりぎりす, is its own answer.

    How are points worked out?

    One point for each letter of a word you submit, so a longer word is worth more: りす is 2 points and きりぎりす is 5. Each hint takes away 1 point.

    Is anything saved or sent?

    No. The puzzle is made in your browser and nothing about your play is stored or uploaded.
