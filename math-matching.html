Five ways to match

  • Make a total: connect two numbers that add to the target. Set the target to 10 for number bonds, or use another value.
  • Take away: connect a starting number to the amount to take away, so the answer is the target.
  • Make a product: connect factor pairs. Each factor pair is shown only once, so 2 × 5 and 5 × 2 are not separate answers.
  • Make a quotient: connect a dividend to a divisor so their division makes the target.
  • Choose an operation: select +, −, × or ÷, then draw a line from the top number to the bottom number to make the target. The operation appears on the line when it is right.

Helpful for touch screens

Tap a first number, then its second number. In three-way mode, tap the answer as the final step. You can also drag from one dot to the next. Any practice line stays on the board, while only a full correct path is counted as solved.
