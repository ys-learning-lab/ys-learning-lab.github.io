How it works

  1. Choose a tool and a starting point. Each generator has one-click templates, such as a math level or a kana set, so you can make a useful sheet in seconds.
  2. Adjust it to fit your child. Change the number of problems, the difficulty, the layout, and the title and teacher name printed on the page. A live preview shows what your settings will produce.
  3. Print a fresh sheet. Generate makes a new PDF every time, with worksheet pages first and the answer key after. Each worksheet page can carry a QR code that shows its answers on a phone.

Who it's for

Homeschooling parents who want practice matched to this week's lesson, not a workbook's schedule. Teachers and tutors who need several versions of the same sheet so students can't copy from each other. Learners of Japanese, of any age, who want steady drills on hiragana and katakana.

Because a new set of problems is generated every time, a child can practice a skill as many times as needed and never see the same sheet twice.

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no account, no subscription, and no limit on how many worksheets you make. Ads on the tool pages cover the cost.

Is my information uploaded anywhere?

No. Worksheets are built entirely in your browser, and the names you type only appear on your printout. See the Privacy Policy.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

Yes. The generators run in any modern browser. For printing, a computer connected to a printer is usually easiest, but you can also save the PDF and print it later.
