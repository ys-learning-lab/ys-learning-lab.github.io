How to play

  1. Look at the grid. Grey squares already hold a letter. The white squares are empty, and the kana below are exactly the letters they need.
  2. Drag a kana into a square. Press a kana with your finger or the mouse, move it over an empty square and let go. You can drag a kana from one square to another, or back down to the other kana. If you prefer, tap a square and then tap a kana.
  3. Spell words from the list. Every across and down run in the grid must be a word from the site's word list. A word turns green when it is full and on the list.
  4. Stuck? Hint fills one square with the right letter and costs 1 point. The number of hints used is shown with your score.
  5. Want it on paper? The Kana Word Puzzle Sheet prints the same kind of crossword with large boxes and an answer key.

Options

  • Words: 5 to 10 words in a puzzle (7 to start). More words make a bigger grid.
  • Letters shown: how many letters of each word start filled in. Few is the hardest, Many the easiest.
  • Sound: a kana says its sound when you pick it up. It starts off; tick the box to hear the kana.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the words come from?

From a short list of everyday hiragana words: the example words Writing Practice uses, plus some longer ones.

Why are there points?

Just for fun. A word that is on the list scores one point for each of its letters, so a longer word is worth more: はし is 2 points and はしら is 3. Each hint you use takes away 1 point.

Is anything saved or sent?

No. The puzzle is made in your browser and nothing about your play is stored or uploaded.
