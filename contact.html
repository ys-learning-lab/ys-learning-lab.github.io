What helps us fix a problem quickly

  • Which page or tool you were using (Math, Kana Conversion Quiz, Kana Matching, Kana Tap Practice, Kana Writing Practice, the Kana Chart, or the Kana Study Plan).
  • For a printed worksheet, the worksheet code printed at the top of the page, such as XRGD0831-A-1.
  • For a wrong answer, the problem number and the answer you expected.
  • For display, sound or printing problems, your browser and device (for example, "Safari on iPad"), and for a printed chart the paper size you chose.
  • If the page has an Export (.ini) button, open the saved file in a text editor and paste its contents into your message, so we can reproduce the exact sheet.

Please don't include a child's full name or other personal details. Messages are only used to reply to you and improve the site. See the Privacy Policy.
