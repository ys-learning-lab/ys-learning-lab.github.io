Online Learning

Kana practice that runs right in the browser, on a phone, tablet or computer: nothing to print and nothing to install. Short rounds are enough, so a few minutes a day adds up.

Kana Tap Practice

On-screen practice for phone and computer: one hiragana, katakana or romaji is shown and the student taps the match. You choose the types, rows and number of choices; the score and every answer appear at the end.

Kana Word Puzzle

A small crossword. Some letters are shown; drag the kana into the empty squares to spell the words. Works with touch and with a mouse.

Kana Word Search

Find the hidden words in a grid of kana. Tap the letters of a word one by one, then press Submit; longer words score more. Works with touch and with a mouse.

What goes here, and what stays on paper

This section is for practice that works well on a screen: recognizing letters and building words by tapping. Writing is different. A finger or a mouse is nowhere near as accurate as a pencil, so writing practice stays on printed sheets.

For the printable tools, the charts and the study plan, go to the Japanese section.
