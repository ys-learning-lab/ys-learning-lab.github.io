How the word search sheet works

Each page is a grid of hiragana with words hidden in straight lines. The student circles the words they find, writes each one on a line under the grid, and scores one point for every letter. The sheet asks for a number of words, but more are hidden than that, so the student chooses, and finding the long words is the way to a high score.

The hidden words are simple everyday words, and a longer word or two is always hidden when the grid is big enough. Any other real word the student finds in the grid is fine too. The filler letters are checked so that they don't spell anything that doesn't belong in a classroom.

Using the sheets

  • When to use it. After the student can read most of the hiragana. It is good practice for scanning for kana and for reading short words.
  • How hard. Start with a 6 × 6 or 7 × 7 grid and "Across and down". A bigger grid, diagonals and backwards words make it harder.
  • Scoring. The student adds up the points at the bottom of the page: one for each letter of every word they wrote.
  • Prefer the screen? The Kana Word Search in Online Learning is the same game, played by tapping the letters.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an answer key?

Yes, as example answers. With "Include example answer pages" on, the PDF ends with one page for each puzzle that rings the hidden words and lists them. Other real words in the grid count too, so a student's list can be different and still right.

Does it include katakana?

Not yet. The word list is hiragana only, so the puzzle is hiragana.

Is anything saved or sent?

No. The puzzles and the PDF are made in your browser, and nothing about them is stored or uploaded.
